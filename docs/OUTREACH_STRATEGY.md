# EthixAI Outreach Strategy

**Target:** Fintechs using AI/ML for lending, credit scoring, underwriting, or fraud detection
**Goal:** First 3 paying customers in 60 days
**Motion:** Free bias assessment → consulting engagement → platform subscription

---

## 1. Target List Criteria

**Ideal Customer Profile (ICP):**
- Fintech with 50-500 employees
- Uses AI/ML for credit decisions, lending, or insurance underwriting
- Series A-C funded (budget exists, but not enterprise bureaucracy)
- Has a "Head of Data Science" or "ML Engineer" title (your buyer)
- Operating in US, UK, or EU (regulatory pressure is your tailwind)

**Disqualifiers:**
- Pure payments (Stripe, Square-type) — no lending = no fairness risk
- Pre-revenue / pre-product — no budget
- Banks with 1000+ employees — too slow, need compliance team approvals

**Where to find them:**
- Crunchbase: Filter "fintech" + "Series A-C" + "AI" or "machine learning"
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator: "Head of Data Science" at fintech companies
- Fintech directories: Fintech Zoom, Fintech News, CB Insights
- Twitter/X: Follow #fintech #responsibleAI hashtags

---

## 2. The Offer (Free Bias Assessment)

**What you're offering:**
> "I'll audit one of your AI models for bias across protected attributes (gender, race, age) and deliver a compliance-ready report in 5 business days — for free."

**What they give you:**
- Access to a sample of predictions (anonymized OK)
- 30-min call to understand the model context
- Permission to publish an anonymized case study (if results are positive)

**Why this works:**
- Low commitment (free, no contract)
- Immediate value (they get a report they'd otherwise pay $10K-50K for)
- Positions you as the expert (not a vendor)
- Natural upsell: "Now let's monitor this continuously with EthixAI"

## 5. Qualifying Questions (On the 30-min Call)

Once they agree to the assessment, ask:

1. "What model are we auditing? Credit scoring? Underwriting? Fraud?"
2. "What protected attributes do you currently test for?"
3. "Have you had any regulatory questions about your AI models?"
4. "What's your current process for model validation?"
5. "Who else is involved in AI governance at [Company]?"

**Red flags:**
- "We don't use AI for decisions" → Not your customer
- "We already have a fairness tool" → Competitor exists, probe harder
- "We're pre-revenue" → No budget, skip

**Green flags:**
- "We're worried about EU AI Act" → Pain is real
- "Our investors asked about bias" → Budget + urgency
- "We don't have a process yet" → Perfect, you're the solution

## 7. Conversion: Assessment → Paid Customer

**After delivering the report:**

1. **Schedule a debrief call** (mandatory, not optional)
2. **Walk through findings** — make them feel the pain
3. **Present the risk** — "If a regulator asks, here's what they'd find"
4. **Offer the solution:**
   - Option A: One-time deep audit + remediation plan ($5K-15K)
   - Option B: Continuous monitoring platform ($500-2K/month)
   - Option C: Both ($10K setup + $1K/month)

**Pricing anchor:**
> "Enterprise fairness tools cost $50K+/year. I'm offering the same capability at $12K/year because I'm building my client base."
