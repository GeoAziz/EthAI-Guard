## Research Checklist

For each company, find:

- [ ] **Head of Data Science / ML** — LinkedIn name + profile URL
- [ ] **CEO / CTO** — LinkedIn name (for warmer intro)
- [ ] **Recent news** — funding, launches, regulatory issues
- [ ] **AI/ML mentions** — Do they talk about AI in their product?
- [ ] **Fairness mentions** — Do they mention fairness, bias, or responsible AI?
- [ ] **Tech stack** — What AI tools do they use? (job postings reveal this)
- [ ] **Contact email** — Find direct email or use firstname@company.com pattern

---

## How to Research Each Company (15 min per company)

1. **Website:** Read homepage + "About" page. Do they mention AI/ML?
2. **LinkedIn:** Search "Head of Data Science" or "ML Engineer" at [Company]
3. **Job postings:** Check if they're hiring ML roles (indicates AI investment)
4. **News:** Google "[Company] AI" or "[Company] fairness" for recent coverage
5. **Crunchbase:** Check funding stage and investors
6. **Twitter/X:** Do they post about AI, fairness, or responsible AI?
